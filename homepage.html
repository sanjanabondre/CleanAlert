About CleanPulse

CleanPulse is a demand-driven waste management system that empowers citizens to report full or overflowing bins via a simple mobile app. Real-time alerts are sent directly to municipal authorities, who can verify reports, view bin statuses, and optimize garbage truck routes based on actual needs — not fixed schedules.

📱 Citizen Mobile App

Report overflowing bins in seconds with photos and GPS support.

🖥️ Municipal Dashboard

Track reports in real-time and manage cleanups efficiently.

🚛 Smart Routing

Garbage trucks dispatched based on need, saving fuel and cost.

⚡ Real-Time Alerts

Instant notifications ensure faster response and cleaner streets.
